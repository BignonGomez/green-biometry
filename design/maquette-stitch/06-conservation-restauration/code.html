Methodological Rigor

The Ecological Monitoring Lifecycle

An open, standardized 8-stage scientific protocol ensuring every recorded restoration outcome is verifiable, reproducible, and ready for global policy synthesis.

STAGE 01 polyline

Spatial Delimitation

High-precision perimeter mapping via GeoJSON & STAC metadata, establishing verified boundary coordinates and land tenure baseline.

STAGE 02 assignment_turned_in

Baseline Characterization

Documenting initial degraded state and benchmarking against nearby reference climax ecosystems (SER 5-Star recovery framework).

STAGE 03 tune

Indicator Selection

Customizing biomarker telemetry metrics: canopy height, species diversity (Shannon-Wiener), soil organic carbon, and blue carbon accretion.

STAGE 04 sensors

Multi-Sensor Ingestion

Coordinating satellite constellations (Sentinel/Landsat), UAV-LiDAR surveys, AudioMoth acoustic nodes, and eDNA sampling runs.

STAGE 05 security

QA & Verification

Topological QA checks, outlier filtering, and cryptographic validation ensuring full compliance with ISO-19115 geographic standards.

STAGE 06 history_toggle_off

Longitudinal Tracking

Multi-year trajectory mapping to capture inter-annual seasonal rhythms, droughts, and long-term biomass succession trends.

STAGE 07 model_training

Biometric Modeling

Algorithmic carbon stock calculation, habitat fragmentation analysis, and faunal carrying capacity predictive simulations.

STAGE 08 public

Open Science Sharing

FAIR-compliant open access data publishing into the Green Biometry index for global conservation policy integration.
