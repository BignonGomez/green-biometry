Éthique & Gouvernance

Scientific Integrity & Participation Principles

Every contributor commits to uncompromising empirical standards, indigenous data rights, and strict biosafety measures.

01

Scientific Rigor & Traceability

All submitted models and telemetry must document sensor calibration protocols, coordinate reference systems, and error margins to allow third-party reproducibility.

02

Open Data & Attribution

Strict adherence to Creative Commons (CC-BY 4.0 / CC0) and FAIR principles ensures that contributors receive full academic attribution while serving global research.

03

Respect for Indigenous Stewardship

Compliance with Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) for localized biocultural knowledge. Traditional ecological stewardship must be recognized and protected.

04

Sensitive Species Protection

Automated coordinate blurring and spatial obfuscation are systematically enforced for endangered fauna and flora prone to illegal trade or poaching.

05

Constructive Peer Collaboration

Discussions, reviews, and errata notices are conducted in an atmosphere of mutual scientific rigor, transparent methodology debates, and collegial respect.

06

Responsible Environmental Evidence

Prohibiting greenwashing, inflated biomass claims, or speculative extrapolations. Findings published on Green Biometry must strictly reflect verified telemetry.
