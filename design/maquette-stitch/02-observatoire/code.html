Data Transparency & Scientific Integrity

From raw Earth data to actionable knowledge

A verifiable four-stage scientific governance framework ensuring traceability, reproducibility, and open access for global planetary observation.

01
cloud_sync
Stage 01

Data collection

Multi-source acquisition from optical & radar satellites (Sentinel, Landsat), airborne LiDAR surveys, and ground-truth IoT acoustic/soil sensors.

02
verified
Stage 02

Source verification

Cryptographic hashing, spatial coordinate validation against ISO 19115 geographic standards, and open provenance tracking.

03
fact_check
Stage 03

Scientific assessment

Rigorous calibration with ground truth, peer review protocols by independent ecological committees, and error margin estimation.

04
public
Stage 04

Publication & visualizer

FAIR principles compliance (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), open OGC web services (WMS/WFS), and public dashboard synthesis.
