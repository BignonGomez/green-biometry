Central African Moist Forest Multi-Decadal Canopy Moisture & Evapotranspiration Baseline (1995–2024)

DOI: 10.1038/s41598-gbio-024 • CC BY 4.0 Open Access

High-precision 10-meter gridded harmonized time-series combining GEDI spaceborne full-waveform LiDAR and Sentinel-2 MSI radiance. Evaluates microclimatic buffering across 1.8M km² in the Congo Basin, calibrated with 24 eddy covariance flux towers.

Provider CNES & Green Biometry
Coverage Congo Basin (Salonga)
Temporal Range 1995 – 2024
Package Size 4.2 GB (Cloud Native)

Sine-Saloum Delta Mangrove Rhizophora Zonation, Soil Salinity & Carbon Stock (2018–2024)

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.gbio-saloum-991 • CC BY-NC 4.0

Multi-spectral drone photogrammetry and acoustic Doppler current profiler (ADCP) benthic surveys across 76 estuarine channels. Includes 420 sediment core samples quantifying deep blue carbon burial rates down to 2.5 meters.

Provider Univ. Cheikh Anta Diop
Coverage Saloum Delta, Senegal
Temporal Range 2018 – 2024
Package Size 1.8 GB GeoParquet
Biodiversity DwC-A Archive FLAC Acoustic verified Taxa Validated

Albertine Rift High-Resolution Drone-LiDAR & Bioacoustic Faunal Census (Nyungwe-Kibira Corridor)

Collection Methodology & Instrumentation

Acquired via heavy-lift hexacopter UAV integrated with a Riegl VUX-120 survey-grade sensor (pulse repetition 2.4 MHz, multi-target capability, < 15mm precision) paired with 28 AudioMoth autonomous acoustic monitoring devices deployed at 500m stratified intervals across high-elevation rainforest canopy.

Provider Institution Albertine Conservation Alliance & Green Biometry Lab Lead PI: Dr. A. Uwimana (Kigali Center for Ecology)
